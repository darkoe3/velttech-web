Velttech Academy Student Learning Guide

Welcome to your learning dashboard. Use it to review your programme, assignments,
attendance, progress, payments, notifications, and learning resources.

Recommended weekly routine:
- Check assignments and due dates.
- Review attendance after each class.
- Track progress reports from your instructor.
- Download resources before class.
- Review payment status and receipts when needed.

For support, contact Velttech Academy through the official contact channels on
the website.
